## 2023-10-29 - MediaCard Keyboard Accessibility
**Learning:** The custom interactive `MediaCard` components were wrapped in non-semantic `div`s with `onClick` handlers, missing keyboard operability and focus indicators. Replacing them with `<article>`, adding `tabIndex={0}`, an `onKeyDown` handler (for Space and Enter), and `focus-visible` ring styles avoids ARIA anti-patterns (like adding `role="button"` on a container with nested buttons) while effectively restoring keyboard functionality.
**Action:** When creating custom clickable cards or similar complex interactive components, favor semantic wrappers (like `<article>`) equipped with `tabIndex` and `onKeyDown` handlers for interactions, without assigning `role="button"`, and ensure visible focus states are present using Tailwind's `focus-visible` classes. Also always remember to label icon-only buttons with `aria-label`.
